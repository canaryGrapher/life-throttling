# Shalimar Bagh: Bliss in Paradise
Shalimar Bagh was built by the Mughals, known now as the _Crown of Srinagar_, was built by Jahangir for his wife in 1619. It was originally name 'Farah Baksh' by the Mughal, but after being under the rule of different rulers, it gots it current name.

It was one of the first trips with my parents that I remember. It was chilling cold and I was about 10. My dad kept saying "smile" and I was bummed about the unfamiliar clothes I was wearing. I wanted to go back into my room and embrace the heat that the extra thick quilt was imparting. 

![Little Yash in Shalimar Bagh](https://ik.imagekit.io/canarygrapher/LifeThrottling/Places/Jammu%20and%20Kashmir/Srinagar/Shalimar%20Bagh_87i4ZzVG_)

I remember playing cricket in the heights of hills and running behind that damn frisbee my mom got me after I threw a spectatable tantrum.

Other memories might be dal, but I do remember the clean air, the times when it was peaceful and a tourist hotspot.
